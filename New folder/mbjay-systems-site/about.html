About BAM Agency

Helping businesses find their momentum and grow.

Why we do it

BAM Agency exists to make those parts work better together, with clear, useful digital tools built around the way each business works.

A few useful answers

Good to know before we start.

Still have a question? Talk to us ↗

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with startups looking to build momentum and established businesses that want to improve how they attract, manage and follow up with customers.

What services do you offer?

Websites and landing pages, CRM and lead-management setup, business automation, and practical AI workflows.

How does a project get started?

Start with a conversation about your business, what is getting in the way, and what you want to improve. We can then agree on a sensible scope and next steps.

Can you work with my existing website or CRM?

Often, yes. We can look at what you already use and discuss whether it makes sense to improve it, connect it to other tools, or replace part of it.

Can we keep working together after launch?

Yes. We can agree on monthly support after handover. Depending on what you need, that can include analytics setup and review, regular website updates, and continued improvements to your CRM and workflows.

How much does a project cost, and how long will it take?

It depends on the scope, features and tools involved. After an initial conversation, we can define the work and provide a project-specific estimate and timeline.
