Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit BAM Agency’s website or contact us.

Information you choose to share

You can contact BAM Agency directly by email or use the project enquiry form. The form runs in your browser and prepares an email with the details you enter; your email app opens so you can review and send it. The information is not sent to BAM Agency by the website until you send that email. We use the details you choose to share to respond and discuss your request. Email is handled by your and our email providers.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted by Vercel. When a page is requested, Vercel may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, and request data to deliver, secure and maintain the hosting service. See Vercel’s privacy policy ↗ for its handling of that information.

Website performance check

If you use the website-check box, the website address you enter is sent to Google PageSpeed Insights to run its report. The report opens on Google’s service, where its privacy practices apply. See Google’s privacy policy ↗.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

As currently configured, this website does not use advertising pixels or a mailing-list signup, and BAM Agency has not enabled website analytics on this site. The optional website-check tool sends the URL you submit to Google PageSpeed Insights. Hosting or browser features may also use or store technical data needed to deliver or protect the site.

How long information is kept

We keep messages for as long as needed to respond, manage the conversation and maintain appropriate business records, then delete or securely dispose of them when they are no longer needed, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us, subject to applicable legal requirements. Contact rodayobj@icloud.com. If you are in Nigeria, you may also raise a data-protection concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

If we add features such as contact forms, social embeds, analytics or marketing tools, we will update this notice to reflect how those features handle information.
